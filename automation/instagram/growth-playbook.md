# Socialora Instagram — Organic Growth Playbook (`@socialora1`)

Updated: `2026-11-11` | Audience: Global English-speaking creators, freelancers, UGC creators, and small brands | Goal: Non-follower discovery (Reels), high-save authority (Carousels), and qualified visits to `getsocialora.com/tools` with **zero spam and zero fake claims**.

---

## 1. Core Algorithm Rules (What Actually Grows `@socialora1`)

Instagram distributes content through two distinct pipelines:
1. **Connected Reach (Followers):** Driven by relationship history, Carousels (Instagram re-serves Slide 2 if Slide 1 is skipped), and quick Story/feed interactions.
2. **Unconnected Reach (Non-Followers via Reels & Explore):** Driven by **3-second hold rate**, **average watch time / loop completion**, **DM Shares ("Sends per reach")**, and **Saves**.

### Format Mix (Priority Order)
- **60% Reels (`1080x1920`, 6–10 seconds, loopable motion graphics + real tool UI):** Primary engine for non-follower reach. Short, tight pacing forces re-reads and replays (>100% completion rate).
- **30% Multi-Slide Carousels (`1080x1350`, 4–5 JPEG slides):** Highest Save rate and dwell time. Each swipe sends a strong positive quality signal.
- **10% Single Infographics (`1080x1350` JPEG):** Fast visual reference cards. Never rely solely on single images for account growth.

## 2. Content Value Ratio (Not a Product Catalog!)

Accounts that only advertise their own tools look like ads and get scrolled past. Follow the **50 / 30 / 20 Creator Value Split**:
- **50% Pure Zero-Pitch Creator Playbooks & Psychology:** No tool URL forced. Actionable hooks, retention fixes, algorithm myths, export checklists, and teardown formulas creators immediately save or DM to a friend.
- **30% Practical Workflow Tutorials Featuring Real Free Tools:** Start with a concrete editing/growth pain point, show the fix visually, and offer the exact browser tool naturally (`getsocialora.com/tools/<slug>` + bio link).
- **20% Contrarian Creator Truths & Community Prompts:** Honest industry takes ("Why 10 saves beat 100 likes", "Why deleting 200-view Reels hurts testing") and genuine comment prompts (`Comment your niche for a 2-second hook idea`).

## 3. Reels Creative & Safe-Zone Standards

Every Reel generated by `automation/instagram/generate_assets.mjs` must pass these visual standards:
- **Dimensions & Format:** `1080x1920` (9:16), `30fps`, `H.264` (`yuv420p`) MP4 with valid AAC audio stream (`anullsrc`), kept lightweight (`< 3 MB`) for fast GitHub/Vercel delivery.
- **Reels Safe Zone:** Keep all critical hooks, subtitles, and UI demos inside the middle vertical band (`y: 260px` to `1520px`, `x: 72px` to `910px`) so the top header, right-side like/comment/share buttons, and bottom caption overlay never hide text.
- **Visual Beat Pacing:**
  - `0.0s – 1.8s`: Bold 4–7 word pattern-interrupt hook + progress indicator.
  - `1.8s – 4.5s`: Visual contrast ("Weak vs Strong" or concrete mistake).
  - `4.5s – 7.5s`: Step-by-step fix or authentic `getsocialora.com` tool preview.
  - `7.5s – 9.0s`: Clear 1-line takeaway + Save/Link-in-bio cue that loops cleanly back to frame 1.
- **Visual Diversity:** Rotate distinctly styled templates (`split_compare_reel`, `checklist_motion_reel`, `myth_vs_fact_reel`, `ui_spotlight_reel`, `framework_steps_reel`) and bright editorial/studio palettes. Never repeat the same neon-purple gradient.

## 4. Human Copywriting, Instagram SEO & Hashtags

### Strict Anti-Robotic Rules
- **NEVER use AI clichés:** *"In today's digital landscape"*, *"Unlock your potential"*, *"Let's dive in"*, *"Game-changer"*, *"Elevate your brand"*, *"Skyrocket"*, *"Unleash"*, *"In conclusion"*, *"Remember that..."*.
- **NEVER make fake claims:** No guaranteed follower counts, no fake analytics screenshots, no fabricated user testimonials.
- **Write like a seasoned creator strategist:** Direct, specific, conversational, crisp line breaks.

### Caption & SEO Blueprint
1. **Hook Line (< 120 characters):** Visible before `...more`. Must state a sharp problem, mistake, or concrete observation using primary search keywords naturally (e.g., *Reels retention*, *3-second hook*, *Instagram bio*, *UGC pricing*).
2. **Value Body (2–4 short lines):** Give the exact "why" or micro-steps so opening `...more` rewards the reader.
3. **One Natural CTA:** Rotate between Save prompt (*"Save this before exporting your next Reel."*), Reply prompt (*"Drop your niche if you want a hook angle."*), or Bio-link pointer when featuring a free tool.
4. **3–5 Relevant Niche Hashtags Only:** Place cleanly at the bottom. Never spam 20–30 generic tags (`#viral #fyp #explorepage`). Example: `#reelstips #contentcreator #instagramgrowth #creatortools`.

## 5. Safe Cadence & Publishing Gates (`Europe/Istanbul`)

- **Active Growth Ramp (2 Posts/Day):** Maximum **2 published posts per Europe/Istanbul day** — prioritizing **1 high-retention multi-scene Reel + 1 high-save Carousel** (or alternating format), with **minimum 5 hours** between posts so each upload completes its non-follower test cohort without cannibalizing the previous one. *(Note: jumping straight to 3–4 IG posts/day on a brand-new account splits Explore testing and triggers quality throttling; only test 3/day after 14+ days of clean retention signals.)*
- **Global EU + US Peak Windows (with Daily Randomized Minutes):** `instagram-publish-workflow.json` checks a local 10-minute jitter gate (`Zaman Penceresi & Dakika Sapması`) so posts publish at **different non-robotic minutes every day** (`:04–:52`) inside peak EU + US usage windows:
  - **Slot 1 (`15:xx–16:xx TSİ`):** UK/EU afternoon break (`12:00–14:30`) + US East morning wakeup (`07:00–09:30 EST`).
  - **Slot 2 (`20:xx–22:xx TSİ`):** EU prime evening (`17:00–20:30`) + US East midday (`12:00–14:30 EST`) + US West morning (`09:00–11:30 PST`).
- **SEO & Discovery Multipliers:** Attach descriptive `alt_text` automatically on Carousel/Image uploads, direct website tool visitors (`getsocialora.com/tools/*`) to `@socialora1`, and pin top 3 flagship posts on the profile grid.
- **Live Duplicate Guard:** `instagram-publish-workflow.json` reads live `@socialora1` recent media via `graph.facebook.com/v21.0` before publishing, blocking both same-day excess (`liveTodayCount >= 2`), spacing violations (`< 5h`), and duplicate captions/IDs.
- **Container Polling Gate:** For `REELS` and `CAROUSEL`, always poll container `status_code` until `FINISHED` before calling `media_publish`.
- **Profile & Bio Alignment:** Keep `@socialora1` Name (`Socialora | Creator Growth & Tools`), 3-line creator-focused bio, and tracked link (`https://www.getsocialora.com/tools?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=profile_link`) aligned with every Reel/Carousel CTA.
